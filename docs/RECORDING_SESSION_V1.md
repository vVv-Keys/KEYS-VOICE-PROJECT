# KEYS Voice Dataset Recording Session v1

**Dataset:** `KEYS_VOICE_MASTER_v1`  
**Target usable material:** 40–60 minutes  
**Capture:** mono WAV, 48,000 Hz, 24-bit  
**Processing:** none  
**Goal:** natural identity, range, articulation, dynamics, emotion, and musical behavior.

## Absolute rules

No Auto-Tune, Melodyne, pitch correction, reverb, delay, chorus, saturation,
mastering, limiting, aggressive compression, doubles, mixed harmonies, Suno
vocals, or instrumental bleed. Preserve the untouched original before cleanup.

## Setup

- Mic distance: 6–8 inches; 10–20 degrees off-axis if plosives are strong.
- Use a pop filter when available and keep the same body/room position.
- Normal peaks: -18 to -10 dBFS; strongest peaks: -10 to -6 dBFS; never clip.
- Record one primary mic/environment. Create separate `ALT_MIC` or `CAR` sets later.
- Stop immediately if higher-register work hurts or produces strain.

## Naming

`KVM1_[CATEGORY]_[NUMBER][TAKE]_[DESCRIPTION].wav`

Examples: `KVM1_MEL_001A_Natural.wav`, `KVM1_RAP_001B_Hard.wav`.

## Session order

Record four 15–20 minute blocks with 5–10 minute breaks:

1. Speech, vowels, and articulation.
2. Natural singing and pitch behavior.
3. Rap, melodic rap, and dynamics.
4. Register, expression, ad-libs, and free performance.

### Pilot rule

Stop after the first 15–20 minutes. Validate format, clipping, noise, mic distance,
and dataset balance before continuing.

## A. Warm-up — do not include by default

- Gentle `mmm`, `mmm-ah`, `mmm-oh`, `mmm-ee`.
- Lip trill: low-middle-low and middle-high-middle.
- Comfortable sirens on `OO`, `EE`, and `AH`, three each.

## B. Speech

### `KVM1_SPK_001_Neutral.wav`

> I didn't know exactly where I was going when I started. Most of the time I

> completely obvious. Other days I have to stop, listen, and find the direction
> again. The important part is that the voice still sounds like me. I don't want
> perfect. I want recognizable. I want something natural enough that nobody has

> perfect. This is just my regular speaking voice when I'm comfortable.
> Sometimes I talk fast. Sometimes I drag words out. Sometimes my voice gets
> quieter at the end of a sentence. That's part of the sound too.

## C. Vowels and articulation

Hold `AH`, `EH`, `EE`, `OH`, and `OO` for 3–5 seconds at quiet, normal, and strong
intensity. Use natural singing vowels, not exaggerated classical shapes.

Record the following words and then the sentences below as
`KVM1_ART_001_Consonants.wav`:

```text
baby maybe better broken paper people perfect pressure picture
dream drive down dark different take time tonight truth turn
stay sound summer say somebody keep cold came could crazy
fade fall feel fire forever
```

> I keep coming back even when I know better. Everything changed but the feeling
> stayed. Tell me the truth even if the truth hurts. Maybe tomorrow won't look
> anything like tonight. I never needed perfect, I just needed something real.

## D. Pitch, sustain, slides, and vibrato

- Sing `1-2-3-4-5-4-3-2-1` on `AH`, `EE`, and `OO` in low, middle, and comfortable upper keys.
- Choose five usable-range notes. Sustain `AH`, `EE`, and `OO` for four seconds,
  first straight and then with natural vibrato.
- Record smooth low-to-high and high-to-low slides on each vowel.
- Record short `1->3`, `3->5`, `5->3`, `3->1`, `1->5`, and `5->1` scoops.
- Record `straight -> vibrato`, `vibrato -> straight`, and uncontrolled natural sustains.

## I. Rhythm, emotion, and dynamics

Perform each line slow, fast, syncopated, laid back, ahead of beat, behind beat,
melodic, spoken, aggressive, and quiet:

- `I knew where this was going before you said a word.`
- `Everything changed when the room went quiet.`

Perform `I never thought we'd end up here` and `Everything is going to change
after tonight` as neutral, happy, sad, hurt, angry, exhausted, confused,
nostalgic, hopeful, detached, intimate, and confident.

Sing `I can hear you calling from the other side` and `I don't know where this
road ends tonight` at very quiet, quiet, medium, strong, and very strong without shouting.

## K. Attacks, releases, and human imperfections

Record `stay`, `me`, `love`, `night`, `gone`, `away`, `home`, and `fire` with
soft, normal, hard, and breathy onsets. Record `stay`, `away`, `tonight`,
`forever`, `home`, and `alone` with clean cutoff, breathy release, falling pitch,

Allow natural breaths, texture, slight rasp, small scoops, cracks, consonant noise,

> Maybe I'm tired of fixing every little thing  
> Maybe the broken edges are part of how I sing  
> Maybe the breath between the words matters too

## Quality review

Flag clipping, crackles, interface pops, fan/AC/traffic, room echo, mic bumps,
notifications, bleed, hum, severe clicks, and plosives. Do not delete automatically.
Mark each take `approved`, `repair`, `exclude`, or `rerecord` in the recording log.

The dataset is done when it includes strong, clean examples of speech, consonants,
vowels, natural singing, melodic and straight rap, all usable registers, head voice,
breathy and strong delivery, vibrato and straight tone, slides, attacks, releases,
dynamics, emotions, ad-libs, imperfections, free performance, and a cohesive song.
